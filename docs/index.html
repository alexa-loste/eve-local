Eve

Your memory,
on your Mac.

Eve remembers what matters to you across your conversations with Claude and ChatGPT. Everything it keeps stays on your computer.

Eve runs on a Mac. Open this page on your Mac to download it.

New to Eve?

Download Eve for your Mac:

Mac with Apple chipM1, M2, M3, M4 and newer Mac with Intel chipOlder Macs

Not sure which? Click the Apple menu, then About This Mac. “Chip: Apple M…” means an Apple chip; “Processor: … Intel” means Intel. Eve is free and needs macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer.

Then three steps

  1. Drag Eve into Applications

    Open the file you downloaded. In the window that appears, drag Eve onto the Applications folder.

  2. Let your Mac open it, once

    Open Eve from Applications. Your Mac will say it couldn't check Eve. Click Done. Then open System Settings, choose Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Eve. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. You only do this the first time.

  3. Click “Set up everything”

    Eve connects to Claude on your Mac and brings your memories over. Then just talk to Claude as usual and ask it to remember things.

Questions

Does it cost anything?
No. Eve is free. When you talk to Claude or ChatGPT, your assistant files your memories as part of the conversation you are already having.
Where are my memories kept?
On your Mac only, with no account to make. You can see, edit and delete every memory in Eve. The one exception: if you type a memory into Eve itself, its text goes to the AI model chosen in Eve's settings, so Eve can organise it.
Which assistants work with it?
Claude Desktop and Claude Code connect with one click. Other assistants that support MCP can connect too: Eve shows you how in Settings.
Comfortable with Terminal? Install in one step

This downloads Eve, checks it, puts it in Applications and opens it, with no permission step:

curl -fsSL https://github.com/alexa-loste/eve-local/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
